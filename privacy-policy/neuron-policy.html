Privacy Policy

Application: NEURON Hospital (Android)

Effective date: 2 November 2026  |  Last updated: 2 November 2026

In brief: NEURON is an application for authorised hospital and clinic personnel. It supports hospital administration and patient-service workflows, including OPD appointments, EEG-related workflows, pre-bookings and follow-ups. It may handle staff account details, patient and appointment information, operational records and device information needed for synchronisation and notifications. It does not sell personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how NEURON Hospital, Latur, Maharashtra, India (“NEURON Hospital”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, receives, uses, stores, shares, retains and protects information in connection with the NEURON Hospital Android application (“NEURON”, “App” or “Application”). It also explains the choices and rights available to people whose information is handled through the App.

This policy applies to the App and its related hospital-operated services used by authorised staff. It should be read together with applicable hospital policies, patient notices, consent documents and legal requirements. If a particular workflow has a separate patient notice or consent process, that process also applies.

Intended users: NEURON is designed primarily for authorised hospital, clinic, reception, administrative and other approved personnel. It is not a public patient portal, does not provide a patient account-registration facility for general public use, and should not be used by unauthorised persons.

1. Who is responsible for the information

NEURON Hospital is responsible for the operation of the NEURON App and for the hospital records and workflows managed through it. For privacy questions or requests, contact the hospital using the contact details in Section 17.

For patient information entered by hospital personnel, NEURON Hospital determines the purposes for which the information is used in its clinical and administrative operations. Google and other technology providers may process limited information on the hospital's behalf to provide infrastructure and app functionality, as described below.

2. Information handled by the App

The exact information handled depends on the user's role, the features used and the information entered into a particular workflow. The App may handle the following categories:

2.1 Patient identity and contact information

  • Patient or child name.
  • Age and age unit, and other basic demographic details entered for a hospital workflow.
  • Address or locality, where entered.
  • WhatsApp number, mobile number or other contact number, where entered.
  • Patient type or category, where used by the hospital.
  • Referring person or referral information, where entered.

2.2 Appointment and hospital-service information

  • OPD appointment and booking details, including appointment date, city, serial or queue details and appointment status.
  • Next follow-up date or city and follow-up information.
  • Pre-booking information, including visiting date, booking time, patient details and booking status.
  • EEG appointment, EEG call and related service workflow details.
  • Service charges, payment mode and payment amounts entered into the relevant workflow.
  • Appointment identifiers, booking request identifiers, mutation identifiers, timestamps and synchronisation status.
  • Other operational details entered by authorised users for the purpose of arranging or managing hospital services.

2.3 Health-related and sensitive patient information

NEURON is used in a pediatric neurology and epilepsy hospital workflow. Depending on what authorised staff enter or retrieve, records may reveal that a person is receiving neurological, epilepsy, EEG or other healthcare services. Users may also enter service-related notes or other health information in fields provided by the hospital workflow. Such information is treated as confidential and should be accessed only by personnel who need it for an authorised hospital purpose.

The App's presence or use does not mean that every listed category is collected from every user or patient. Information is handled when it is entered, received from the hospital backend or required for a feature.

2.4 Staff and account information

  • Staff login username and password.
  • Assigned city, role, administrative status and feature-access permissions.
  • Session identifiers and login/authentication status.
  • Account-management actions performed by authorised administrators, such as adding, updating, importing or removing staff access credentials.

Staff accounts are provisioned and managed by authorised hospital administrators. The App is not intended to allow members of the general public to create their own staff accounts.

2.5 Device, app and technical information

  • A randomly generated App installation identifier used to associate a particular installation with device registration and notification operations.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token used to route push notifications to the installation.
  • App version and operational state information used for compatibility, synchronisation and troubleshooting.
  • Booking request IDs, event IDs, timestamps, retry and synchronisation status, and similar technical records used to prevent duplicate operations and reconcile results.
  • Network connectivity state, to determine whether network-dependent operations can run.

The App does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos or media, or precise GPS location through the permissions declared in the reviewed Android application manifest. If a future version introduces a new permission or data category, this policy and the relevant in-app disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

3. How information is obtained

Information may be obtained in these ways:

  • Direct entry by authorised staff: for example, when staff create or update an OPD booking, pre-booking, EEG workflow or follow-up record.
  • From the NEURON backend: the App retrieves appointment lists, follow-up records, schedules, configuration, user permissions and other operational information from the hospital's backend.
  • From the Android device: the App obtains the installation identifier, notification registration token, connectivity state and, if permission is granted, approximate location information for the city-detection feature.
  • From push messages: Firebase Cloud Messaging may deliver event or response data needed to refresh operational records, confirm an operation or show a relevant notification.

4. How and why information is used

Information is used only for purposes connected with operating the App and the hospital workflows it supports, including:

  • Authenticating hospital personnel and checking their assigned access permissions.
  • Creating, updating, confirming, retrieving and managing OPD appointments, pre-bookings, follow-ups and EEG-related workflows.
  • Displaying patient lists, appointment details, schedules, operational statistics and reports to authorised users.
  • Synchronising information between the App, the hospital backend and authorised devices.
  • Sending relevant booking confirmations, operational alerts, administrative messages and data-refresh notifications.
  • Associating notification registrations with an app installation and handling device logout or registration changes.
  • Detecting the approximate city of a device, when the user grants permission, to warn when the city selected in the App differs from the detected city.
  • Preventing duplicate or inconsistent booking operations, recovering interrupted requests and verifying whether a submitted operation succeeded.
  • Maintaining, securing, troubleshooting and improving the reliability of the App and its backend workflows.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, clinical record-keeping and hospital administration requirements.

We do not use patient or staff information to sell advertising, build advertising profiles or sell personal information to data brokers.

5. Location permission and city detection

The App declares Android's approximate location permission, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. It is used for a limited feature: determining the approximate city where the device is located so that the App can display a city-mismatch warning when the user has selected a different visiting city.

  • The feature is designed to determine a supported city, not to track a user's movements.
  • The App uses the Android location provider and Android Geocoder to obtain a location result and resolve it to a city name.
  • The implementation may use a recent cached location or request a single location update when needed.
  • Raw latitude and longitude are held temporarily in memory while the city is resolved. The reviewed implementation does not persist, log or transmit those raw coordinates to the NEURON backend.
  • The resolved supported city and the time/status of the check may be used locally for the city-mismatch feature.
  • Location permission is not required for the core appointment and hospital workflow features.

Android's location provider, Geocoder implementation or related platform services may process location information to return a location or address result. Such processing is governed by the relevant Android/device provider and Google service terms and privacy practices. We do not control the internal processing performed by those platform services.

You can deny or revoke approximate location permission through Android Settings. If permission is denied, device location is disabled or a city cannot be resolved, the city-mismatch feature may not be available. The App should continue to provide its other functions that do not depend on this feature.

6. Device storage, background operation and Android permissions

6.1 Local storage

The App uses Android private app storage and a local Room/SQLite database to support app operation, offline access, pending requests, cached appointment and follow-up information, synchronisation and recovery of interrupted operations. Depending on the workflow, local records may include patient names, age, contact details, address, city, appointment details, EEG/pre-booking details, request identifiers and operational status.

Local copies are used to display information and to reconcile it with the hospital backend. A record stored locally may remain on the device until it is synchronised, refreshed, cleared by the App, removed through an app data reset or deleted when the App's data is removed. Logging out does not necessarily mean that every locally cached operational record is immediately erased.

6.2 Login information on the device

The App stores login/session information in app-private preferences to support authentication and re-authorisation of an existing staff session. Access to app-private storage is restricted by Android's application sandbox. However, app-private storage is not the same as a guarantee of encryption against every form of device compromise or backup exposure. Users should protect their devices and credentials, and the hospital should ensure that its production configuration and backup settings meet its security requirements.

6.3 Android permissions and system functions

The reviewed App manifest declares the following permissions or system capabilities:

  • Internet: to communicate with the NEURON backend and required service providers.
  • Network state: to determine whether network connectivity is available for synchronisation and other online operations.
  • Approximate location: for the city-mismatch warning described in Section 5.
  • Notifications: to display operational notifications. On supported Android versions, the user can grant or deny notification permission.
  • Receive boot completed: to support eligible background work or notification-related tasks after the device restarts.
  • Ignore battery optimisations request: the App may ask the user to exempt it from certain battery restrictions to improve the reliability of background synchronisation and notifications. This is optional and controlled through Android settings.

Permission availability and behaviour depend on Android version, device manufacturer and user settings. Users can manage permissions in Android Settings. Disabling a permission may limit the related feature without necessarily disabling unrelated App features.

6.4 Android backup

The reviewed application manifest enables Android app backup. Depending on the Android version, device configuration, user's Google account and applicable Android backup rules, some app-private data may be included in device backup or transfer. Backup handling is controlled in part by Android and the device backup configuration. Users should secure their device and Google account. NEURON Hospital should review and configure backup exclusions for sensitive local patient and credential data before production distribution.

7. Hospital backend, transmission and synchronisation

The App communicates with a NEURON Hospital-operated backend implemented using Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. The backend receives information submitted by authorised staff and provides records and configuration needed by the App.

  • Data is transmitted to the configured backend over HTTPS.
  • Requests may include the action being performed, patient or appointment details relevant to that action, login/session information where authentication is required, and request or installation identifiers.
  • The backend is used to store and manage hospital operational records, process bookings and updates, maintain user permissions, and provide data for synchronisation.
  • Background workers may synchronise eligible records when network connectivity is available. Some data may remain in local storage until a later successful synchronisation.
  • Request IDs, event IDs, timestamps and status information may be used to identify the correct operation, prevent duplicate processing and recover from interrupted network requests.

Network failures may delay synchronisation or notification delivery. Users should verify important appointment and patient information in the authorised hospital workflow rather than assuming that an offline change has already reached the backend.

Access to backend records is intended to be limited to authorised hospital users and administrators. The exact retention, backup, access-log and deletion behaviour of the Google Sheets/Apps Script backend is managed separately from the Android client. Contact NEURON Hospital for questions about a particular hospital record.

8. Push notifications and Firebase Cloud Messaging

NEURON uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications and data messages to registered App installations. FCM is provided by Google.

  • The App obtains an FCM registration token and associates it with a randomly generated installation identifier.
  • The token and installation identifier are sent to the NEURON backend for device registration and notification routing.
  • When a user logs out, the App may send a device logout/unregistration request so that the backend can update the device's registration state.
  • Messages may contain event types, request identifiers, appointment identifiers, city/date information, account-access responses or other data required to refresh or confirm an operational workflow.
  • Some event messages may include limited patient or appointment information needed to display an operational notification. The exact content depends on the event and backend message configuration.
  • Notifications may be delayed, disabled by the user or device, or not delivered due to network, operating-system or service-provider conditions.

Notification categories are controlled in the App and Android settings. Users can disable notifications through Android Settings; doing so may prevent alerts from appearing, although some background data synchronisation may continue where required by the workflow.

Google may process FCM tokens, delivery metadata and message data as necessary to provide its messaging service. See Google's and Firebase's privacy information in Section 15.

9. Sharing and disclosure of information

We do not sell, rent or trade patient, staff or other personal information. Information may be disclosed or made available only in the following circumstances:

  • Authorised hospital personnel: staff and administrators may access records according to their assigned responsibilities and permissions.
  • Technology and infrastructure providers: Google services, including Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Android/Google platform services, may process information necessary to provide hosting, storage, messaging, geocoding or app functionality.
  • Legal requirements: information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, a court order, lawful government request or other valid legal process.
  • Safety, security and rights: information may be used or disclosed when reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, misuse, security incidents, unauthorised access or threats to patients, staff, the hospital or others.
  • Business or service changes: if the operation of the App or its supporting services is reorganised or transferred, information may be transferred only subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and legal requirements.

We do not authorise technology providers to use patient information for their own advertising purposes. Their processing is subject to their service terms and privacy commitments.

10. Data retention, correction and deletion

We retain information for the period reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including hospital operations, continuity of care, appointment management, administrative requirements, dispute resolution, security and applicable legal or professional record-keeping obligations.

  • Hospital records: appointment, follow-up, EEG, pre-booking and related records may be retained in the hospital backend in accordance with hospital record-retention practices and applicable requirements.
  • Local device records: cached and pending data may remain on a device until it is synchronised, refreshed, cleared by the App or removed through Android app-data controls.
  • Account information: staff account details and access records may be retained while the account is active and for a reasonable period after deactivation where needed for security, audit or legal purposes.
  • Notification registrations: FCM registration information may be retained while an installation is registered or until the backend processes an applicable logout, replacement or removal request.
  • Technical records: request identifiers, status records and related operational data may be retained as needed to reconcile bookings, investigate failures and maintain service integrity.

Deleting the App or clearing its local data does not automatically delete information already transmitted to or stored in the hospital backend. Logging out does not itself constitute a request to erase a patient's hospital record.

10.1 Requesting correction or deletion

A patient, parent/guardian where appropriate, or authorised representative may contact NEURON Hospital to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification, applicable law, clinical record-keeping duties and other legitimate retention requirements. We may need additional information to locate and verify the relevant record.

Where a record must be retained under applicable law or professional obligations, we may be unable to delete it, but will consider whether an appropriate correction, restriction or annotation can be made.

10.2 Staff account deactivation

Staff accounts are managed by authorised hospital administrators. A staff member who no longer requires access should request deactivation through the hospital administrator. Deactivation is intended to stop future access; it does not automatically erase historical hospital records or records that must be retained for operational, audit or legal purposes.

11. Information security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. These measures include app-level authentication, role/permission-based access, Android application sandboxing, HTTPS communication with the configured backend, request validation and operational controls for synchronisation and notification handling.

No app, network, device, storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Security also depends on the device, operating system, account configuration, backend administration and user practices.

Authorised users must:

  • Keep usernames, passwords and devices secure.
  • Never share credentials or allow unauthorised people to use a signed-in App.
  • Use the App only for legitimate hospital-related purposes.
  • Lock devices when unattended and log out where appropriate, especially on shared devices.
  • Avoid taking screenshots, copying, exporting or disclosing patient information except where expressly authorised.
  • Promptly report a lost device, suspected account compromise, incorrect disclosure or other security incident to hospital administration.

12. Children and patient information

NEURON is a staff-facing hospital operations application, not an app designed for children to register or use independently. Authorised hospital personnel may enter or access information about patients who are minors, including children receiving pediatric neurology or epilepsy services, when needed for legitimate hospital workflows.

Patient information about children must be handled with appropriate confidentiality and in accordance with applicable law, professional obligations, hospital policies and any required parent/guardian authorisation or consent process. The App should not be installed or used by a child for independent access to hospital systems.

13. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on the person's relationship to the information and applicable law, the person or their authorised representative may contact NEURON Hospital to:

  • Ask whether personal information is being handled through the App or hospital workflow.
  • Request access to or a copy of information, where legally available.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request erasure or deletion where applicable and where retention is not legally or operationally required.
  • Raise an objection or concern about a particular use or disclosure.
  • Withdraw a permission granted to the Android App, such as approximate location or notifications, through device settings.
  • Request staff account deactivation through the hospital administrator.

We may verify identity and authority before responding, especially where a request concerns a child's or another person's patient record. We may ask for clarification or decline a request to the extent permitted or required by applicable law. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
